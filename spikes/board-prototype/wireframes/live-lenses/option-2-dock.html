What is where

Three panes, all existing. The sidebar is unchanged. The chat dock (the app's one chat slot) is pointed at the selected seat's T3 thread instead of the review's own thread — its header keeps the trail and gains a back link. The main region carries the bench compressed to a five-row rail across its top, with the selected lane's board filling everything below it. Flagged shows its two voices as indented rows under the lane, because a lane with two seats has two threads.

Board elements and transcript receipts are tied by the letters a–d. In a built version that tie is a shared highlight: hovering a receipt lights its element and vice versa, and an element pulses once as its receipt lands. Element c is drawn heavier on both sides to stand for “this one just arrived”; the dashed boxes are where the next element and the next transcript entry will appear.

On click

Clicking a lane row on the rail selects that lens: the board below the rail swaps to that lens's board, and the dock swaps to that lane's seat thread. Clicking one of Flagged's two voice rows selects the Flagged board and that voice's thread — the board is shared, the transcript is not. The top bar's lens switcher does the same thing from the centre slot, so there are two equivalent controls; the rail is the one you can reach while reading. Clicking “Back to the session” in the dock returns the dock to the review's own conversation and leaves the board where it is. Closing the chat with the top bar's toggle hides the transcript entirely and gives the board the full width.

Cost against the current shell

Lowest of the four — most of this already exists. packages/app-ui/src/app/preparation-bench.tsx already models five lanes with per-seat voices (voicesOf names them Claude/Codex when a lane has more than one seat) and already makes every reader a control that calls uiActions.openLensThread({ reviewId, thread }) and opens the dock. packages/app-ui/src/chat/t3-chat-dock.tsx already renders slot.thread … readOnly with a “← Back to the session” button when ui.lensThread is set. packages/app-ui/src/shell/top-bar.tsx needs no change at all: the trail already hands off to the dock header when the dock is open.

The new work is the main region: the bench has to compress from its full-height five-reader scene into a rail and mount the selected board underneath, in the same outlet. The known limits are that ui.lensThread holds one ref, so opening a seat evicts the session thread until you come back, and the dock is narrow — a transcript with prose, tool output and receipts in ~360px will wrap hard.
