Designer notes  /  what to weigh in on
1
One map of the flow. Projects list to add-project to the narrated processing, then the two-zone project detail, the review canvases, one draft, one signed paper.
2
Two entry points. Your own local work (backlight, heading toward Make PR) and a teammate’s PR (ink, into teammate review). Both land in the same canvases.
3
The re-steer loop. A Yours branch can loop back to the coding harness with your requested changes before it ever becomes a PR.
4
The material legend. Ink publishes, blue stays local, glass is chrome, paper is the one thing you sign. The whole app reads by material.
