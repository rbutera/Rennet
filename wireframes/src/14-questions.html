Questions: the orchestrator, by default

Ask
orchestrator-only default ask both is an opt-in split

Every question goes to the orchestrator by default. The send control carries one small split: ask both models, remembered per thread, never sticky globally. When you ask both, the answers arrive as two labeled cards, side by side. There is no synthesis block, ever: the robotic auto-merge is gone.

Designer notes  /  what to weigh in on
1
Orchestrator by default. Every question goes to the one harness you converse with. Nothing auto-fires to a second model behind your back.
2
Ask both is a split, not a mode. A small caret on the send control offers “ask both models”, remembered per thread and never sticky globally.
3
Two answers, labeled. When both are asked, you get two cards side by side, each named by its model. You read them yourself.
4
No synthesis, ever. Rennet never manufactures a merged answer. If the two disagree, that disagreement is itself something you can ask the orchestrator about.
