Designer notes  /  what to weigh in on
1
One index for the machine’s opinions. Model-council findings and dual-review disagreements, in one queue. This is the lens that repairs Decisions by subtraction: everything machine-opinionated leaves it.
2
Severity plus agreement state. Each flag says how bad and how sure: both models concur, or they disagree, with the vote counts.
3
Disagreement lives here. When the models split, both answers sit side by side in this lens, labeled. It no longer flares up mid-conversation in the chat.
4
The lens is an index, not a house. The flags still render as marks at their anchors on the code surfaces; this lens is the thing that jumps you to them.
