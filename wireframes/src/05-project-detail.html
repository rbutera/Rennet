Designer notes  /  what to weigh in on
1
Two zones, one scroll. No tabs as primary structure. Seeing your in-flight work and the team’s PRs in one glance is the whole point of the screen.
2
Yours is backlight, and it is a pipeline. Blue means private to this machine. The trajectory dots read captured › reviewed › PR’d, so a row shows where in the local pipeline it sits.
3
The terminal verbs. A Yours row’s action is stage-dependent and ends in Make PR (the paper ceremony) or Re-steer (hand the changes back to the harness).
4
Team is ink; your PRs live here too. Once a branch has a PR, the PR row wins and the worktree becomes a glyph on it (see #477). One item, one row, no double-listing.
5
Chips switch; the glyph gates. The Yours / Team chips are a soft switcher (click one to collapse the other). The title-bar mode glyph replaces the old consent banner entirely.
