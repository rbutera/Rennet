The one solid object. It previews exactly what will post, nothing editable. The verdict is derived from your dispositions (here, request changes) and shown with its derivation, and you can override it. The Paper is a crumb child of the Draft — the crumb reads … › Draft › Paper, and back lands on the Draft, because editing lives there. The human sign carries the real verdict: the sign in the loop is the safety, not a forced-neutral event.

Designer notes  /  what to weigh in on
1
The one solid object. Paper is opaque: it shows precisely the comments that will land on the PR, in the order they will land. Editing happened on the draft; here it is frozen.
2
The verdict is derived. Rennet reads your dispositions and derives Approve / Request changes / Comment, and shows the arithmetic (2 request-changes, 1 comment).
3
Override is yours. The derived verdict is a default, not a lock. You can change it before signing; the review carries whatever you sign.
4
The sign is the safety. Rennet never forces a neutral event. The human sign in the loop is the safeguard, and it publishes the real verdict.
