Digest is the read path. The phone leads with the delta digest — the desk’s tall canvas becomes counts + rows + one finding at a time.
2
Size ceiling by design. Hunks render to a ceiling with “open full canvas”; the survey’s RN perf tail says never stream a big diff into a list.

Judgement is one tap. Agree / disagree / discuss and proposal adjudication are thumb-sized — the unit acts of triage.
